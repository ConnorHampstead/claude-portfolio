# Disclaimer

This repository is a public record of an experiment testing whether a large
language model can produce calibrated, profitable trading decisions. It is not
investment advice.

- **No real money is involved.** Every order goes to an Alpaca paper trading
  account. Nothing here is a real position and no capital is at risk.
- **The trade ideas are machine-generated and unreviewed.** They are written by
  an AI model running unattended on a schedule, with no human checking them
  before submission. They are published because publishing predictions before
  outcomes are known is the only way to make the experiment honest — not because
  they are worth acting on.
- **Do not trade on anything in this repository.** Simulated fills flatter results
  substantially: no slippage, no queue position, no partial fills, and no
  emotional pressure. A profitable paper record here would not imply a profitable
  live one.
- **Losing calls are not deleted.** That is the point. Commit history is the
  experimental record, and pruning it would destroy the only thing that makes the
  numbers meaningful.

If you are looking for financial advice, speak to a licensed advisor.
