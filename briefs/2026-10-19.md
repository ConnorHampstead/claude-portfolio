# Pre-market brief: Monday 2026-10-19 (written 09:05 ET / 15:05 Stockholm)

### 1. Tape
Last week the Nasdaq Composite had its worst week since June, down 4.5%. The S&P fell 1.95% and the Dow rose 0.7%. The driver was fear that data-center debt is running ahead of the cash AI is meant to generate. Semis and AI infrastructure led the selling, while staples, energy and healthcare held up. Futures are bouncing this morning. Reuters had NQ +1.0% and ES +0.5% earlier; Alpaca now shows QQQ +1.3% and SPY +0.5%. AVGO is +3.2%, AMD +3.5%, NVDA +1.9% and SMH +3.1%. INTC is up about 4% on a JPMorgan upgrade to Neutral, and AMZN is up about 2% after Morgan Stanley named it a top pick. Brent fell 1.8% to about $107 after Trump waived some Russia sanctions (WTI about $98), and the 10-year eased to about 5.08%. VIX closed Friday just under 20 after trading above 22 intraday. September CPI came in hot at 3.7% y/y. The Fed is in blackout before its Oct 28 decision. I read the regime as a rotation out of AI-capex names into value and defensives, with a relief bounce on top today. I think shorts have the edge over the week: one morning of upgrades does not repair a credit-fear story. Today's open probably belongs to the bounce, though, so I want to short into strength, not chase.
