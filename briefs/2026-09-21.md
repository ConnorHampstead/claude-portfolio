# Pre-Market Brief — Monday, 2026-09-21

*Written ~13:05 UTC / 15:05 Stockholm, before the US open. Cash session today 15:30–22:00 Stockholm (EDT = Stockholm − 6h).*

### 1. Tape

The week opens **risk-on, and the fulcrum is oil.** Crude is down for a fourth consecutive session — WTI ~$97.1 (−3.2), Brent ~$100.5–101.7 (−2.1% to −3%) — on three separate legs: Saudi loadings partially recovering via Oman despite continuing Houthi attacks, product inventories building in the US, Singapore and Europe alongside higher Chinese fuel exports, and hope that the Iran war gets a diplomatic window at UNGA this week (*all verified*). Layered on top, Treasury Secretary Bessent called weekend US–China talks "very successful" ahead of Thursday's Trump–Xi summit in Washington, so futures are up across the board: Dow +401 (+0.77%) to 52,480, S&P +54.25 (+0.70%) to 7,766, Nasdaq +334.50 (+1.12%) to 30,251, with chips leading. VIX closed Friday at **14.81** (down from 17.71 on Fed day), 10-year at **4.95%**, gold $4,386 (−$38.90).

The regime is a **geopolitical risk-premium unwind**, not a fundamental re-rating. That matters because it tells you where the asymmetry is: the things that were *broken by* $100+ crude and a closed Strait of Hormuz are far from their highs, while the things that led the tape into today's gap (semis, crypto proxies) are not. The Fed hiked 25bp to 3.75–4.00% on Sep 16 explicitly to fight oil-led inflation — if crude genuinely deflates, that hike thesis weakens, which is a second-order tailwind for the same beaten-up cyclicals. Today favors **one selective trade on a pullback**, not chasing the gap. Today's own calendar is empty; the week's risk is entirely headline, not data.

I am treating the UNGA headline flow as ambient two-sided risk and have priced it into a 46% probability rather than pretending it away.
