### 1. Tape
October CPI came in hot at 08:30 ET. Headline was +0.4% m/m against 0.3% expected, and core was +0.3% against 0.2%. Core landed exactly on the 0.3% line Governor Waller set this morning, above which he says "a 50 bp hike should be considered." Markets now price about 84% odds of a 25 bp December hike and 15% odds of 50 bp. The 5-year yield is up 10 bp to 4.75% and the 10-year up 7 bp to 4.93%. That is a bear flattener led by the front end, and it hurts long-duration and rate-sensitive equities most. Futures have given back part of Monday's +2.35% rally: QQQ is −1.2% pre-market at 671, SPY −0.6%, IWM −0.9% and SMH −2.2%. The Dow is flat. Brent is under $90 for the first time since August after Trump said no further Iran strikes are needed, so USO is −3.0% and XLE −1.2%. Gold is −2.0% as real yields rise. The regime is a hawkish repricing on top of a rally that was driven by short-covering. **I think shorts have the edge today, mainly in rate-sensitive beta and in energy, which is trending lower.** Monday's rip is a warning that dip-buying is strong, so I am using half and probe tiers, not core size.
