# Pre-market brief — Monday 2026-09-28 (written 09:05 ET / 15:05 Stockholm)

## 1. Tape

Futures are lower into a risk-off Monday. S&P futures are about −0.3% to −0.5% and Nasdaq-100 futures about −0.5% to −1.0% (sources differ by time of print). SPY last traded 768.78 (−0.3%) and QQQ 740.17 (−0.6%).

- **What's driving it:** President Trump rejected Iran's conditional peace proposal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz. WTI is up about 4% to around $96 and Brent is near $108 (RTE, EnergyNow, CNBC headline). Rates add pressure: the 10-year is about 5.22–5.23%, which reports describe as a 19-year high, and TLT sits at its 20-day low (78.93 against a low of 78.83).
- **Volatility and gold:** VIX is about 16.0 (+7.7%). Gold fell about 3% (GLD 380.09, below its 20-day low of 388.39), which hit Newmont.
- **Single names:** Tech leadership is being sold after last week's run (META −1.2% after +13% on the week, AMD −1.0%, MSFT −1.3%). The exception is NVDA, up 1.8% on a $150B buyback increase, the largest single authorization on record. The software "AI displacement" selloff continued on Friday (ZS −10%, TWLO −8%, OKTA −5.5%), CRM is −4.1% pre-market, and ORCL is −3.4% on fallout from Project Jupiter plus layoffs.
- **Regime:** Rates and oil are the drivers and breadth is poor. Rate-sensitive groups (IWM, XLU, XLRE, XHB, HD) and financials sit at or near their 20-day lows, and the big-cap AI names hold up the index.
- **Edge today: short.** The rate-sensitive and broken names have the edge, with one long in the one name that has a dated positive catalyst.
