## Patterns

1. **The stated probabilities are consistently too high.** Trades said to be about 40% likely hit target 25% of the time (8 trades), and passed ideas said to be 38% likely hit 29% (14 ideas). Combined, 23 ideas were said to be 38% likely and came true 27% of the time. The stated `p_target_first` has drifted down from 0.45-0.52 in August to 0.28-0.38 in October. *Inferred:* the model may already be correcting, but realised hit rates are still below what it states.
2. **Stop entries rarely trigger.** All 13 never-filled proposals were stop (breakout) entries. Stop entries filled 9 of 22 times (41%; MSFT is open and left out), while limit entries filled 5 of 5. The overall fill rate is 54% (15/28), down from 65% on 10-06, and only 1 of the 9 proposals since 10-06 filled. The README treats a rate below 50% as a sign of unrealistic levels. Stop entries that did fill returned -2.47R over 9 trades; limit entries returned -0.59R over 5.
3. **Shorting defensive names has gone 0 for 4.** UNH, XLRE, JNJ and WMT shorts all stopped out (-4.05R together), and an XLU short never filled. The shorts that won (BAC, HD) were financials and consumer discretionary. The sample is small, so treat this as a lead, not a conclusion.
4. **Conviction 2 trades are 0 for 4** (BA, UNH, WMT, CAT; avg -0.86R). Conviction 3 trades are 3 for 10 (avg -0.24R), and the one conviction 4 trade (LLY) is the best result so far. These samples are too small to conclude anything, but conviction 2 is worth watching.
5. **Losses are mostly held to about 1R, with overnight gaps as the exception.** Only ROST (-1.18R) and IWM (-1.16R) lost more than 1R. IWM gapped through its 276.90 stop before the open on 10-08. The DAL brief from 09-21 had already named an overnight gap through a tight stop as its main risk.
